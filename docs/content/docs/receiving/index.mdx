The receiving module is where goods physically arriving at the facility are
counted in against their [purchase orders](/docs/purchasing/purchase-orders) and
become new stock. It closes the procure-to-receive loop: purchasing commits to
buying an item, and receiving turns the delivery that follows into countable
inventory.

Every receipt does two things at once. It records that some or all of a purchase
order has been delivered, advancing the order's status; and it creates a new
**lot** of the item, stored in the item's inventory unit of measurement, ready
to be tracked, transacted, and consumed like any other stock.

## Where it fits

Receiving sits between purchasing and inventory:

1. A [purchase order](/docs/purchasing/purchase-orders) is confirmed with a
   supplier and moves to **Confirmed / Awaiting Delivery**. Only then does it
   appear in receiving.
2. When the shipment arrives, you receive its line items: fully, or partially if
   only some of the order turned up.
3. Each received line becomes a [lot](/docs/inventory/item-details#lots) of the
   item, and the order advances to **Received** or **Partially Received**.
4. Optionally, you print QR [labels](/docs/receiving/labels) for the new lots so
   the physical stock can be identified and scanned later.

The receiving dashboard lives at `/receiving`, and is also reachable from the
**Receiving** button on a purchase order and the **Receivable POs** panel on the
home dashboard.
