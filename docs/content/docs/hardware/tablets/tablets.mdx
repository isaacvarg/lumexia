Lumexia is currently only a web application, so any tablet with access a web browser should work.

However, different tablets have seen more success on deployments/usage of Lumexia due to durability in warehouse and speed.

## Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 or (A11+)

These have been very successful in both laboratory and warehouse usage. The responsiveness and speed doesn't create a bottleneck that leaves staff feeling slowed down. This tablet has also connected with scanners and printers quite well.

![Galaxy Tablet](/modules/hardware/galaxy-tab.jpg)

###  Accessories

The following are also highly recommended to pair with this tablet:
- Multi-device charger
  - Placing the devices on a dedicated charging station at the end of the day keeps Lumexia available during work hours
- Case
  - Bright colors really helps with visibility in the warehouse and keeps them from getting lost 
  - These cases have been great for protecting devices in warehouse conditions
  - The screen protector is not used due to the increased tactile / latency introduced. However, the case is quite sturdy and still offers good protection.
  - [Amazon](https://amazon.com/dp/B0CHNS45D6)

![Case](/modules/hardware/galaxy-case.jpg)

## Tried, but **not** recommended

### Amazon Fire Tablets

While cheaper than other tablets, the responsiveness of the screen left warehouse staff feeling frustrated and slowed down.

Additionally, IT departments had varying degrees of success with trying to pair their scanners.
