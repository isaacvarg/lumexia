---
title: Scales
description: How weights get into Lumexia — entered by hand, not read from a balance.
---

Lumexia works with weights throughout — inventory amounts, transactions, and
production quantities are all measured values. But it does **not** connect to a scale
or balance. You read the value off your device and **type it in**.

## Entering weights

Wherever Lumexia asks for a weight or amount, it's a number field you fill in
yourself. The main places this happens:

- **Inventory** — item amounts and stock [transactions](/docs/inventory/quantities),
  including the counts you enter during an [audit](/docs/inventory/audits).
- **Production** — [compounding](/docs/production/compounding) quantities as you work
  through a batch.

Units are handled by [UoM conversions](/docs/inventory/uom-conversions), so you can
enter a measurement in a convenient unit and let Lumexia reconcile it against the
item's base unit.

<Callout>
  Entered weights aren't silent overwrites. Adjustments and counts are written as
  [transactions](/docs/inventory/quantities) against the lot, so on-hand is recomputed
  from the ledger and every change stays traceable.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  There is no serial, Bluetooth, or Web Serial integration with balances today.
  Lumexia does not capture weight automatically from a device — every weight is keyed
  in by a person.
</Callout>
