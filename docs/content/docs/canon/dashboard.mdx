The **Canon dashboard**, at `/quality/canon`, is the system-wide view of
canon's health: how complete the data is, what's waiting on a decision, and
what needs attention. A **Canon settings** link in the header jumps to
[Canon Settings](/docs/canon/data-types) for configuration.

## Stat cards

Four cards summarize the current state at a glance:

- **Coverage** — the percentage of applicable subjects that have a current,
  accepted value.
- **Needs attention** — artifacts in any non-current status.
- **Waiting for review** — open change requests across the whole system.
- **Waiting for you** — open change requests you can personally approve or
  reject.

## Tabs

- **Attention** — a table of every artifact not in **Current** status,
  filterable by status and data type. Clicking a row jumps to that item's
  Canon tab.
- **Requests** — every open change request: its number (CR-), data type,
  subject, kind (Edit, Stale Confirmation, or Source Acceptance), requester,
  current approval count, and age.
- **Coverage** — a per-data-type table, grouped by [group](/docs/canon/data-types#groups),
  with a progress bar of covered vs. applicable subjects and counts of
  Current, Needs attention, Pending, and Missing. Each row expands to list
  the specific subjects still missing a value.
- **Activity** — a chronological feed of events across every artifact.

## The home dashboard panel

A **Canon Reviews** panel is also available on the main Lumexia home
dashboard, surfacing your own **Waiting for you** change requests and any
artifacts needing attention without having to open the full Canon dashboard.
