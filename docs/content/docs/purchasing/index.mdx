The purchasing module covers the full procure-to-receive cycle: someone asks for
an item, a buyer turns that ask into an order with a supplier, and the goods are
later booked into stock by [receiving](/docs/receiving). Everything in the module
hangs off two records: the **request** (a need for an item) and the **purchase
order** (a commitment to buy it from a specific supplier).

## The flow

A typical purchase moves through three stages:

1. **Procurement request:** a user flags that an item needs to be bought. This can
   come from the warehouse, from production planning, from a reordering rule, or
   from an automated trigger.
2. **Purchase order:** a buyer selects a supplier, adds the items and quantities,
   and pushes the order through its stages until it is confirmed and awaiting
   delivery.
3. **Receiving:** when the shipment arrives, the goods are counted in against the
   order and become new lots of stock.

Suppliers tie it together. Each purchase order belongs to one supplier, and the
supplier record accumulates a history of everything bought from them.
