A **supplier** is a vendor you buy from. Every purchase order belongs to one
supplier, and the supplier record gathers a full history of what you have bought,
who you deal with there, and how they refer to your items. Suppliers live at
`/purchasing/suppliers`.

![Supplier details example](/modules/purchasing/supplier.png)

## Adding a supplier

Create a supplier from the suppliers list. The form asks for one required field:

- **Name:** the supplier's name. This is all that is needed to create the record.

Expanding **Add additional details** reveals optional fields you can fill in now or
later: **Street 1**, **Street 2**, **City**, **State**, **Zipcode**, and **Phone**.
Leaving them blank is fine. Address and phone, along with whether the address prints
on purchase orders, can always be edited afterward on the supplier's Settings tab.

## The suppliers list

The list shows every active supplier, sorted by name, with search across the table.
Archived suppliers are excluded from the list. Clicking a supplier opens its detail
page.

## Supplier details

A supplier's detail page is organized into six tabs.

### Purchases

The default tab, and the home for spend analytics. A metrics panel with an **All /
This Year / Last Year** toggle shows three figures: **Purchase Orders** (the count
of orders in range), **Total Spent**, and **Avg. Order Value** (total spent divided
by order count), alongside a chart of spend over time. Below the metrics is a table
of every purchase order for the supplier, filterable by status and payment method,
with each row linking to the order.

### Contacts

The people you deal with at the supplier. Each contact has a name and an optional
role, phone, and email. Selecting a contact shows their detail and a dedicated notes
section, so you can keep a running record of conversations with that specific
person.

![Contacts](/modules/purchasing/contacts.png)

### Notes

Supplier-level notes, kept separately from contact notes. These are general notes
about the supplier as a whole. Each note shows its created and updated times and can
be edited or deleted.

### Items

A view of everything you have ever purchased from the supplier, answering how much
and how often you buy each item. The left side lists the items supplied, sortable by
name, quantity, or spend. Selecting an item shows a summary for the chosen date
range: **Total Spent**, **Last Price** per unit, the **units** it was purchased in,
and the number of **purchases**, plus a price trend chart and a table of the orders
for that item.

![Supplied item](/modules/purchasing/supplied.png)

### Aliases

The item [aliases](/docs/inventory/items#aliases) tied to this supplier, meaning the
names or codes the supplier uses for your items. Each row shows the alias name, the
item it maps to, the item's code, and the alias type, and links back to the
inventory item. These aliases are what print on purchase orders sent to the
supplier. Aliases are created on the item itself, as described in the inventory
documentation.

### Settings

Where you maintain the supplier's own details after creation: the address fields,
phone, and a **Show address on purchase order** toggle that controls whether the
supplier's address is printed on generated POs. This tab also holds **Archive
Supplier**.

<Callout>
  Archiving a supplier is a soft delete. The supplier is hidden from the list and
  from new purchase orders, but its history is preserved rather than erased.
</Callout>
