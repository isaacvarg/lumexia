title: Audits
description: Verifying that recorded stock matches what's physically on the shelf.

Recorded stock and physical stock drift apart over time — spills, miscounts,
breakage, and data-entry slips all add up. An **inventory audit** is how Lumexia
reconciles the two: staff physically count what's on the shelf and correct the
records to match.

Lumexia offers two forms of audit:

- **Audit requests** — a focused check of a **single item**, raised on demand or
  automatically.
- **Discrepancy audits** — a broader **category-wide sweep** that counts many
  items in one sitting.

Both are reached from the **Audit** area in the sidebar, whose button shows a
live count of open audits so the warehouse always knows how much verification
work is waiting.

## Open audits

An **audit request** is a standing ask for someone to verify an item's on-hand
quantity. It has a simple lifecycle: it is **open** from the moment it's raised
until an audit is performed against it, at which point it becomes **completed**.
The number badge next to the **Audit** sidebar button counts the open ones,
alerting warehouse staff that verification has been requested.

The **Open** tab of the Audit page lists every open request — showing the item,
who requested it, and when — next to a panel of recently completed audits. The
**Archive** tab holds the full history of completed audits.

Requests are raised in several ways:

| Source | How it happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Reordering rules** | A rule with *Create audit request* enabled raises one automatically when stock runs low. Evaluated daily — see [Reordering Rule](/docs/inventory/item-details#reordering-rule). |
| **Automated audit triggers** | A scheduled job raises requests based on configurable conditions (low on-hand, heavy production usage, negative stock). See [Automated audit triggers](#automated-audit-triggers) below. |
| **Manually from an item** | The **Inventory Audit** action on the [item details page](/docs/inventory/item-details) requests a count for that item. |
| **Manually from production or purchasing** | Planners and buyers can request an audit from a batch's bill of materials or from a purchasing request, alerting the warehouse to confirm stock before they proceed. |

<Callout>
  Automatically raised requests are attributed to the **Lumexia** system user, so
  it's clear the request came from a rule rather than a person.
</Callout>

## Scanning and reviewing on hand

Audits are built for the warehouse floor. From the Audit page, staff **scan a
lot's barcode** with a handheld scanner — no typing, no searching. The scan takes
them straight to that item's audit view.

That view lists every lot of the item alongside the quantity Lumexia currently
has **on record**, with a running total across the lots. Depleted (zero) lots are
hidden by default; a **Show All** toggle reveals them. For each lot, staff compare
the recorded figure against the physical count and take one of two actions:

- **Adjust** — enter the actual counted quantity. Lumexia computes the difference
  and records it as a stock adjustment.
- **Deplete** — zero the lot out when there's physically nothing left.

<Callout>
  Corrections are never silent overwrites. Every adjustment or depletion is
  written as a **transaction** against the lot, so on-hand is recomputed from the
  ledger and the change is fully traceable. See
  [Inventory Quantities](/docs/inventory/quantities) for how stock is derived from
  transactions.
</Callout>

## What an audit does

Performing an audit does more than nudge a number. When an audit request is
**completed**, Lumexia:

- **Reconciles** recorded stock against the physical count through the adjustment
  and depletion transactions the auditor made.
- **Records who and when** — the audit captures the person who conducted it and
  the time it happened.
- **Snapshots every change** — each correction is stored with its before-and-after
  quantity, so the audit is a self-contained record of exactly what changed.

The request then flips to **completed**, and the result joins the item's
[Audits history](/docs/inventory/item-details#audits) and the Archive tab, where
it can be reviewed later in full.

## Discrepancy audits

A **discrepancy audit** widens the lens from one item to a whole category. Rather
than verifying a single item on request, it's a deliberate physical-inventory
sweep of many items at once.

The workflow is:

1. **Start** — choose an item category to audit (or all purchased items). Lumexia
   takes a **starting snapshot** of every item in scope — its total on-hand
   quantity, its number of lots, and how many of those are depleted — and builds
   a worklist with each item marked *not checked*.
2. **Conduct** — working through the warehouse, staff **scan a lot** to pull up
   the item being counted, then adjust, deplete, zero out depleted lots, or
   generate a new lot as the physical count requires. Each item is marked
   **audited** as it's finished.
3. **Close** — closing the audit records an **ending snapshot** for every item.
   Any item still left unchecked is flagged **incomplete**, and a confirmation
   warns how many remain before you close.

### Benefits

Because a discrepancy audit captures a **starting and ending snapshot** for each
item — quantity, lot count, and depleted-lot count — it measures how far an entire
category has drifted from reality in a single, coordinated effort. It gives you:

- **Full coverage** — a structured worklist ensures every item in the category is
  accounted for, with clear *audited* / *incomplete* progress tracking.
- **A measurable before/after** — the paired snapshots quantify the total
  correction across the sweep, not just item by item.
- **A complete trail** — every adjustment is a transaction, so the whole audit is
  auditable after the fact.

## Automated audit triggers

Beyond manual requests, Lumexia can raise audit requests on its own. The
**Triggers** tab of [Inventory Settings](/docs/inventory/items#configuration)
controls this scheduled job:

- **Low on-hand** — flag an item whose stock has fallen below a set fraction of
  its lot's original quantity, when it has no active reordering rule to catch it.
- **Heavy production usage** — flag an item consumed across more than a set number
  of batch records.
- **Negative stock** — flag any lot that has gone negative.
- **Item categories** — enable or disable the trigger per item category, so only
  the categories you care about are swept.

Together these keep the warehouse ahead of stock problems without anyone having to
remember to ask for a count.
