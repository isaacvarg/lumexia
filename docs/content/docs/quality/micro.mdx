The **Micro** area handles microbiological testing, which is typically sent out
to a lab rather than measured in-house. Instead of recording results the way an
[examination](/docs/quality/examinations) does, Micro generates a **Sample
Submission Form (SSF)**: the paperwork that accompanies the physical samples to
the testing lab.

Micro lives at `/quality/micro`.

{/* ![Micro submission](/modules/quality/micro.png) */}

## Creating a submission

Creating a submission is a two-step wizard:

1. **Select a BPR**: search for and pick the produced
   [batch](/docs/production/planning) the samples are drawn from. Each result
   shows the produced item, the batch reference, and its lot number.
2. **Sample designation**: declare how many containers of each kind are being
   sampled: **Drums**, **Pails**, **Gallons**, and **Tank**.

Submitting generates the Sample Submission Form as a printable PDF for the samples
to travel with.

<Callout>
  Micro does not record measurements or evaluate specifications: it produces the
  lab submission document. Results that come back from the lab are recorded
  separately, for example as a [finished-product examination](/docs/quality/examinations)
  on the lot.
</Callout>
