Planning is where **Batch Production Records (BPRs)** are created and managed. A
BPR is one concrete run of an item, instantiated from that item's
[MBPR](/docs/production/mbpr) at a chosen batch size. This page covers requesting
a batch, reading its material sufficiency, allocating stock, scheduling, and
moving it through its lifecycle.

The planning dashboard lives at `/production/planning`.

![Planning dashboard](/modules/production/production-planning.png)

## The planning dashboard

The dashboard has two views:

- **Status**: BPRs grouped by status. A row of status buttons (each with a count)
  sits above a grid of batch cards for the selected status.
- **Table**: every BPR in one table, filterable by **Item** and **Status**.

Two buttons sit in the header:

- **Request Batch** starts a new BPR (below).
- **Handle Completed BPRS** processes finished batches (below).

Clicking a batch card or table row opens that BPR's detail page.

## Requesting a batch

**Request Batch** opens a short wizard: pick the **produced item**, then its
**active MBPR**, then a **batch size** defined on that MBPR. Submitting creates a
new BPR in **Draft** status and does the setup work automatically:

- Copies the MBPR's bill of materials into the BPR, converting each line's
  concentration into an absolute quantity in pounds for the chosen batch size.
- Generates the BPR's per-run steps and actionables from the MBPR's steps.
- Creates a [lot](/docs/inventory/lot-numbers) for the produced item (starting at
  zero quantity) that the finished batch will fill.

"Requesting a batch" therefore means instantiating a ready-to-work draft from the
master recipe. From there, planning works its material sufficiency and schedules
it.

## The BPR detail page

![BPR Details](/modules/production/bpr-example.png)

An open BPR is organized into tabs:

- **Basics**: three cards, **Batch Size**, **Status**, and **Scheduling**, plus
  the **Bill of Materials** sufficiency table (below).
- **Steps**: each batch step and its actionables, with completion values as they
  are filled in during compounding.
- **Quality**: the [examinations](/docs/quality/examinations) conducted against
  this batch.
- **Notes**: notes attached to the BPR.
- **Activity**: the change log.

The **Status** card shows the batch's current status and opens a **Change Status
To…** dialog for manual changes. **Scheduling** sets the batch's scheduled
date range, which is what puts it on the [compounding](/docs/production/compounding)
schedule.

## The bill of materials and material sufficiency

The Basics tab's bill-of-materials table lists every material the batch needs and
whether there is enough stock. Its columns change with the batch's status: a
**Draft** BPR shows the required and available amounts and a sufficiency verdict,
and once the batch leaves Draft the table adds **Staged**, **1° Verification**,
and **2° Verification** columns that track staging progress on the floor.

The **Sufficiency** column flags each material at a glance:

- A green check: enough on hand.
- A yellow triangle: enough on hand for this batch, but not once other draft
  batches are also accounted for.
- A red X: not enough on hand.

Each material line also carries its own status as it is staged: **Pending →
Staged → Primary Verified → Secondary Verified → Consumed**.

### The material allocations dialog

![Allocations dialog](/modules/production/allocations.png)

Clicking a material row opens the material's **Material Allocations** dialog, which breaks
down exactly where the item stands. Every amount is in pounds and carries a
tooltip:

| Amount | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **On Hand** | Physical quantity in stock across all lots. |
| **Allocated (Other Batches)** | Committed to other confirmed batches that have not yet consumed their materials. Excludes this batch. |
| **Soft Allocated (Other Batches)** | Committed to other **draft** batches not yet confirmed. Excludes this batch. |
| **Required for this Batch** | How much this batch needs. |
| **Available after this Batch** | On Hand minus other batches' allocations minus this batch's requirement: what would remain free. |
| **Soft Availability after this Batch** | The above, minus other drafts' soft allocations. |

These mirror the item's inventory quantities; see
[Inventory Quantities](/docs/inventory/quantities) for the underlying
definitions. The dialog also lists active purchasing requests, allocations, and
purchase orders for the material, and lets you act on a shortfall directly:

- **Request Inventory Audit**: raise an [audit](/docs/inventory/audits) to confirm
  the true on-hand count.
- Raise a [purchasing request](/docs/purchasing/procurement-requests) for the
  material.

## Scheduling

Set a scheduled date range on the **Scheduling** card. A batch cannot be opened
on the [compounding](/docs/production/compounding) floor until it has a scheduled
date, so scheduling here is what makes a queued batch workable.

## The BPR lifecycle

Most status changes happen automatically as work completes; the manual "Change
Status To…" dialog is for exceptions. The everyday flow:

1. **Draft**: created by Request Batch. Planning works material sufficiency and
   may set intermediate statuses (Allocating Materials, Verifying BOM
   Fulfillment, Awaiting Materials, Known Material Arrival) while stock is sorted
   out.
2. **Queued**: confirmed and scheduled, ready to stage.
3. **Staging Materials**: materials are staged and verified on the floor.
4. **Compounding**: every material verified; the steps are worked.
5. **Completed**: all steps done.
6. **Awaiting QC**: after the completion cascade consumes materials (below).
7. **Released**: cleared for use or sale.

Off the happy path are **Investigating / Corrective Actions**, **Failed**, and
**Consumption Error**.

## Handling completed batches

<Callout>
A cron / automation to handle pushing the BPR through the workflow with validation is in-progress.
</Callout>

When a batch finishes compounding it becomes **Completed**, but its materials are
not consumed from inventory until you run the completion step. **Handle Completed
BPRS** on the dashboard processes every completed batch at once:

- Creates inventory consumption [transactions](/docs/inventory/quantities) for
  each staged material.
- Marks the batch's materials as consumed.
- Advances each batch from **Completed** to **Awaiting QC**.

If consumption fails for a batch, it is moved to **Consumption Error** and a note
records the cause; a per-batch **Retry Consumption** button on the Status card
re-runs the process once the problem is fixed. The action is safe to re-run: it
cleans up any prior consumption first.
