Compounding is the shop-floor half of production: the place where staff
physically stage the materials for a batch and then work through its steps to
make it. It picks up where [Planning](/docs/production/planning) leaves off, once
a batch is queued and scheduled.

The compounding area lives at `/production/compounding`.

![Compounding](/modules/production/compounding.png)

## Staging and compounding

The work happens in two phases:

- **Staging**: gathering and weighing each raw material the batch needs,
  scanning its lot, and recording the weight. Staged materials are then verified
  by [Production Quality](/docs/production/quality).
- **Compounding**: following the batch's ordered steps: reading the
  instructions, adding the verified materials, and checking off each actionable
  to actually produce the batch.

## The compounding dashboard

The dashboard shows two things:

- A **This Week / Next Week** schedule grid of batches due in the production days
  ahead.
- **All Compoundables**: every batch currently in a workable status.

A batch appears as compoundable when its status is **Queued**, **Staging
Materials**, or **Compounding**.

<Callout type="warn">
  A batch with no scheduled date cannot be opened here: clicking it shows
  **Missing Schedule Date** and asks you to add a schedule date to access it.
  Scheduling is done back in [Planning](/docs/production/planning) on the batch's
  Scheduling card.
</Callout>

Opening a batch shows the right view for its status: a queued batch offers
**Begin Staging**; a staging batch shows the staging panels; a compounding batch
shows the steps.

## Roles

Compounding is built around three roles, and what you see when you open a batch
depends on which role you hold:

- **Production**: stages materials and executes the batch steps.
- **Production Quality**: performs **primary** verification of staged materials
  and flagged steps.
- **Production Quality Secondary**: performs an independent **secondary**
  verification.

If your role has nothing to do at the batch's current stage, you see a read-only
overview that names the team the batch is waiting on. This split enforces that no
single person both stages a material and clears it. Verification is covered in
full on the [Production Quality](/docs/production/quality) page.

## Staging a material

![Staging](/modules/production/staging.png)

From a queued batch, press **Begin Staging** to move it to **Staging Materials**.
The batch's materials are then split into **Not Started** and **Staged**.

To stage a material, open it and work the short wizard:

1. **Scan** the lot's barcode. The scanned lot must be a lot of the required
   item, or Lumexia rejects it.
2. **Enter the weight** in pounds. There must be enough on hand, and the staged
   amount must fall within a **±1.5% tolerance** of the required quantity.
3. Optionally attach a **photo** of the staged material.

Each staged weight is recorded, and a material can be staged in more than one
pull until its total is within tolerance. Once a material is fully staged, its
line moves to **Staged**, ready for [verification](/docs/production/quality).

## Compounding the batch

When every material has been staged and verified, the batch moves to
**Compounding** and the steps open up. Steps are worked in order. On the current
step, staff:

- Read the **instructions**, **materials**, and any **addendums** carried over
  from the [MBPR](/docs/production/mbpr).
- **Check off each material** as it is added. A material can only be checked off
  once it has been **secondary verified**; until then it shows "Awaiting
  verification".
- **Complete each actionable**, entering the value its type calls for (a yes/no
  check, a number, a photo, or a note).

**Complete Step** becomes available only when the prior steps are done, every
material is checked off, and every actionable is complete. When the last step is
completed, the batch is automatically marked **Completed**.

<Callout>
  A step can be **locked** if the previous step still has actionables awaiting
  Production Quality verification. A locked step shows "STEP IS LOCKED" and asks
  you to get Production QA to verify before continuing. See
  [Production Quality](/docs/production/quality) for how this gating works.
</Callout>

## Active timers

The compounding area is designed to track **active timers** for batches in
progress (for example, tank time). This feature is a **work in progress**: the
panel currently shows "No active timers" and is not yet functional.

## After completion

A completed batch still has to have its materials consumed from inventory and be
moved into quality control. That final step is handled from Planning; see
[Handling completed batches](/docs/production/planning#handling-completed-batches).
