Production Quality is the verification checkpoint inside a batch. As materials are
[staged](/docs/production/compounding) and steps are worked, Production Quality
independently checks that the right lot was pulled in the right amount, and that
flagged steps were done correctly, before the batch is allowed to move on.

<Callout>
  This is the **production-side** quality check that gates a batch during
  manufacturing. It is separate from the top-level [Quality](/docs/quality)
  module, which defines item specifications and records QC examinations against
  lots. Both are "quality", but they do different jobs.
</Callout>

## Why verification exists

Every material weighed by production is checked by someone else before it is
consumed, and the check is done **twice**, by two different roles. This two-person,
two-pass control means no single person both stages a material and clears it: a
mistake in the wrong lot or the wrong amount is caught before it goes into the
batch. Certain compounding steps can also require sign-off before the next step
proceeds.

## The verification queues

The dashboard at `/production/quality` gathers the work waiting on quality into
two queues:

- **Awaiting Primary Verification**: staged materials and flagged steps that need
  their first check.
- **Awaiting Secondary Verification**: items that passed primary and need the
  independent second check.

When both queues are empty, there is nothing waiting on quality.

## The verification workflow

Opening an item shows what was staged: the material, the **lot**, the **quantity**
weighed, who staged it, and (in the newer view) the staging photos. The verifier
then either:

- **Approves / verifies** the staged weight, or
- **Denies** it with a note explaining why.

Verification runs in two passes. A staged material advances **Staged → Primary
Verified → Secondary Verified**, with primary done by the **Production Quality**
role and secondary by the **Production Quality Secondary** role. A material's
line is only fully cleared once every staged pull for it has been secondary
verified.

Flagged compounding-step actionables are verified the same way: an actionable
marked as requiring verification is checked (and, if configured, secondarily
checked) before its step can be completed.

## How verification locks the batch

Verification is a gate, not a formality. It blocks the batch's progress in three
places until sign-off is complete:

1. **Staging to compounding.** A batch cannot leave **Staging Materials** for
   **Compounding** until **every** material line has been **secondary verified**.
   Until then the compounding steps do not open.
2. **Step locking.** During compounding, a step is **locked** while the previous
   step has verification-required actionables that quality has not yet verified.
   A locked step shows "STEP IS LOCKED" and directs staff to get Production QA to
   verify.
3. **Material check-off.** A material can only be checked off on a step once it
   has been secondary verified; before that it shows "Awaiting verification".

Together these ensure a batch physically cannot advance past a material or a
flagged step until Production Quality has signed off on it.
