The accounting module is where the money side of a purchase is squared away: it
reconciles a purchase order against its paperwork and payment, decides what a
finished product should sell for, and keeps the catalog of payment methods those
purchases are charged to. It sits downstream of purchasing and receiving; a
purchase order raised elsewhere in Lumexia flows into accounting to be matched,
paid, and (for the items it brought in) re-priced.

The module is organized into three areas, each with its own page.

<Cards>
  <Card title="PO Matching" href="/docs/accounting/po-matching" description="Reconcile a purchase order against its invoice, packing slip, and payment; keep digital copies of the paperwork; and track who did what." />
  <Card title="Pricing" href="/docs/accounting/pricing" description="Run a pricing examination that recomputes consumer prices to hit a target profit margin, then send it through an approval workflow." />
  <Card title="Payments" href="/docs/accounting/payments" description="Maintain the catalog of payment methods (cards, bank transfers, checks) that purchase orders are charged to." />
</Cards>

Like much of Lumexia, this module is shaped around the needs of a cosmetic and
bath-product manufacturer, so some of its vocabulary and checklists are specific
to that workflow.

<Callout type="info" title="Future improvements">
  Today the module covers PO matching, pricing, and payment methods. These pieces
  are robust, but the accounting area still has room to grow: account
  reconciliation, exporting to QuickBooks or other platforms, and similar
  integrations are all on the wish list. If you have suggestions, please open an
  issue on GitHub.
</Callout>
